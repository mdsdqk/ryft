1 Schema editing affordance

The Schema sub-sheet is one always-editable view (settled). Question: how present are the per-row controls? Sample card is users — 6 columns, 2 indexes, 1 constraint. A real branch runs 3–40 such cards, tables up to ~40 columns. The per-row △N marker is the V0 op-log feedback (decided; persistent side panel is V1).

Option A — reveal on row selection

recommended

Row shows its read-only spec by default; click or Enter expands it in place to the edit controls, one row open at a time. + card stays a readable schema; low visual noise at 40 rows; one clear focus target for keyboard. − one extra keystroke to reach a control; needs a visible affordance that the row is editable.

Option B — always-on inline controls

most direct / noisiest

Every row renders its name field, type select, and drop button at all times — no mode, no reveal. + zero interaction cost; every control is always where you expect it. − a 40-column table is 120+ live form controls; the card stops reading as a schema and reads as a form.

Option C — hover / focus reveal

lightest / weak on touch

Row shows its spec; an action cluster (rename · retype · drop) appears on hover or keyboard focus, each opening a small inline field. + card reads as a schema at rest; no expand step. − no hover on touch; the row must reserve space or reflow when the cluster appears; discoverability rests on hover.
